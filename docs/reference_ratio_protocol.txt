Reference size and calibration follow-up
========================================

This follow-up was proposed after inspection of the earlier calibration
results. It tests a new hypothesis. Earlier prospective outcomes are discovery
data for this hypothesis. The protocol and numerical expectations are saved
before any new forecast cells or new A/B outcomes in this experiment.

Fixed setting
-------------
All three corpora use the existing 50,257-token vocabulary, 64-token chunks,
radius 2, smoothing 5, auxiliary weights, 128 evaluation chunks, and saved
mask at q=0.5. Sources are stories, WikiText paragraphs, or news articles.
News retains at most two spaced chunks per article. Source IDs, full-text
hashes, and exact chunk hashes remain separate across roles. We retain the
existing sampling and final-source truncation conventions.

Primary metric
--------------
Calibration is mean forecast divided by arithmetic mean observed squared
disagreement. Each evaluation chunk has equal weight after averaging its
hidden positions. A/B pairs have equal weight. Target calibration is one.
No multiplicative calibration is learned. Full source, source with same-lag
terms only, and the original smoothed multinomial approximation are reported.
The latter estimates its conditional collision at the reference size.

Numerical predictions, fixed before new cells
-------------------------------------------
For the full source method, the observed discovery ranges suggest:
  m/n = 1: calibration in [0.82, 0.88]
  m/n = 2: calibration in [0.93, 0.96]
  m/n = 3: calibration in [0.97, 1.03]
The ratio-three interval is an explicitly chosen tolerance around the three
discovery values at n=2048. These are descriptive prediction ranges, not
confidence intervals or guaranteed bounds. We report exact misses, including
small misses at interval boundaries. We also report absolute distance from
one and absolute log error without converting these into significance tests.

For m/n=4 at n=1024 and 2048, the secondary prediction is that calibration
will be within [0.95, 1.10]. Larger ratios 8 and 16 are stress checks with no
numerical success range. No range is adjusted after seeing new results.

Stage 1: additional forecasts, known A/B outcomes
------------------------------------------------
Use the fixed three matched pairs and existing nested reference bank.
New (n,m) cells per corpus are:
  (512,512), (512,1024), (1024,1024),
  (512,4096), (1024,4096), (2048,4096),
  (512,8192), (1024,8192), (2048,8192).
We also compute (2048,6144), providing ratio three at the same target size
under the matched protocol. Previously computed cells are reused only after
hash and numerical reconciliation. All planned cells will be reported.
These are new computations on known outcomes, not independent prospective
replications. Banks are nested and sizes within pairs are nested.

Stage 2: fresh A/B outcomes
-------------------------
The cached 64,000-source pools have 25,635 unused TinyStories sources, 21,663
unused WikiText sources, and 19,284 unused news sources after historical
exclusions. Allocate three new mutually source-disjoint A/B pairs per corpus,
4,096 chunks per side. Their 2,048-chunk prefixes give a paired size test.

Extend the existing 8,192-chunk reference by 4,096 unused chunks to 12,288.
Allocate a separate fresh 4,096-chunk reference, disjoint from that bank and
all A/B, auxiliary, and evaluation sources. All reference allocations and
forecasts are frozen before scoring any fresh A/B reconstructor. Allocation
uses a fixed seed, without inspecting the resulting disagreement.

Primary nested-reference cells are (2048,2048), (2048,4096), (2048,6144),
(2048,8192), (4096,4096), (4096,8192), (4096,12288).
The separate reference tests (2048,4096) and (4096,4096). It supplies a limited
check of reference composition at one size. Two banks do not support a
population-level confidence interval for reference uncertainty.

Use seed 20261017 plus corpus index for source allocation. Reserve all
historical roles, including unused historical pair sides and reference banks.
Allocate the reference extension, the separate reference, then fresh pairs
from one shuffled eligible source list, excluding each completed role.
If the source pool cannot supply the planned chunks, report the shortage

All absolute forecasts and their file hashes must be saved before any fresh
A/B outcomes are calculated. Timestamped local manifests document sequence.
This is a prospective follow-up to an exploratory hypothesis, not external
preregistration. We report every planned cell and both reference banks.

Uncertainty
-----------
Keep pair identity through aggregation. Conditional intervals for the ratio
use a t interval for the arithmetic mean observed disagreement, inverted
against the fixed positive forecast. Bounds are F/U and F/L when L>0.
Three-pair intervals are imprecise. They condition on the reference,
auxiliary data, and evaluation inputs. Chunks, nested sizes, reference
prefixes, and repeated forecasts are not independent replications.
The older seven-pair results are recomputed using the same ratio definition.

Mechanism check
---------------
Partition masked positions by the smallest raw reference row count among
their visible offsets: 0, 1-4, 5-19, 20-99, and at least 100. A separate
category covers positions without visible offsets. Count each position once.
Retain all lag cross terms within its forecast. Give each position weight
1/(128 times the number of hidden positions in its evaluation chunk).
Stratum sums must reconstruct total predicted and observed disagreement.
Report energy shares and signed contributions, including empty/zero strata.
This checks association with coverage. It does not identify a unique causal
mechanism. Changes of strata with reference size will be stated explicitly.

The existing known-population control remains a separate regime (K=512,
L=8). Compare total calibration with total calibration. Its reference-error
component alone is not comparable to the real-text total ratio. Its opposite
reference and propagation errors prohibit a universal m/n interpretation.

Reporting
---------
Present all previous and new conditions in a common table and figure inputs.
Distinguish discovery, new forecasts on known outcomes, and fresh outcomes.
Do not promote a universal reference-size rule or rewrite the abstract around
the pattern unless the new evidence supports it. Keep the image/text
correspondence table in the main text. Restore prospective calibration to the
main figure and show the complete reference-size comparison.
